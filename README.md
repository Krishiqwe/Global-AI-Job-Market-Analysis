## 🌐 Global AI Job Market Analysis

A comprehensive data analysis project exploring the evolving landscape 
of AI-related jobs worldwide. This project dives into job postings, 
salary distributions, required skills, experience levels, and geographic 
hiring trends to surface actionable insights for job seekers, recruiters, 
and researchers in the AI domain.

### 🔍 Key Objectives
- Analyze salary trends across AI roles, industries, and regions
- Identify the most in-demand skills and tools in the AI job market
- Explore the distribution of remote vs. on-site opportunities
- Compare entry-level vs. senior-level compensation globally
- Visualize hiring hotspots and top-paying countries/cities
